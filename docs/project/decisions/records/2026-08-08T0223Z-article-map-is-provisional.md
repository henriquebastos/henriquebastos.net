# The article map is provisional

## Question

What authority should the Petrus article map have over future editorial work?

## Decision

The map is a bird's-eye hypothesis about conceptual boundaries,
relationships, and tensions. It is not the territory, a backlog, a sequence,
or a commitment to implement every visible article.

Only the article currently being sculpted is a unit of commitment. Work on one
article at a time. Let evidence and concrete reading determine its boundary,
depth, and exclusions. After acceptance, revisit the map and propose changes
based on what the article taught; do not force the article to satisfy the map.

## Rationale

Initial research exposed many plausible essays and relationships, but at low
resolution. Treating that inventory as a plan would freeze abstractions before
they survived concrete editorial work and encourage oversized articles.

Progressive sculpture gives reality room to sharpen the terrain. A completed
article can reveal that topics merge, split, disappear, or connect differently
than the first map suggested.

## Consequences

- Map titles and edges remain provisional.
- No publication order is implied.
- Every article must be self-contained.
- Adjacent ideas that cause drift are parked rather than included merely to
  satisfy the map.
- The post-acceptance handoff includes proposed map changes, clearly separated
  from accepted article content.
- The Navigator rules on those proposals in the map context.

## Review Trigger

Review after each accepted article or when new authoritative evidence creates
a real contradiction in the current map.
