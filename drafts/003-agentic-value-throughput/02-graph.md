# Claim graph: Agentic Value Throughput

Status: Preserved canonical graph from the experimental process. The selected
route is in [02-reading-order.md](02-reading-order.md), and the complete
pre-review draft is in [03-draft.md](03-draft.md). The graph remains as planning
evidence, not as an author-review surface.

The list began as an exhaustive flat inventory. IDs record collection order,
not argumentative order or importance. Compound candidate IDs were retired
when their dependencies touched different parts of their claims. Diagnosis
then removed two restatements, parked one explicitly adjacent branch, corrected
the direction of the value-loop dependency, and transitively reduced the
edges. The move inventory below groups the remaining claims by argumentative
work without changing their dependencies or placing them in prose order.
