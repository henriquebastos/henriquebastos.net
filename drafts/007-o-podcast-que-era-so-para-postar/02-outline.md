# Outline

Each node states what the section must leave in the reader's head. Facts come from the
timeline in the podcast conduit (`01-expand/linha-do-tempo.md`), with line references to
the session.

1. I recorded a conversation with Alisson meaning only to post it, and on a whim handed
   it to an agent to see whether AI would make any difference.
2. The first obstacles were banal (a Drive link behind a login, a transcriber that
   switched to English), and the agent kept moving and told me what it decided alone.
3. The agent could not watch or hear the video, so it checked its work in other ways,
   and I became the only one who could judge the result.
4. The conversation with the agent happened in Obsidian pages more than in the chat:
   I dictated, answered inside the pages, and approved by item number.
5. The look came from a slow sequence (concept, style, details), with my taste as the
   filter and the agent's numbers as the instrument.
6. Sound and the cold open were decided by ear, which is the one sense I had and it did not.
7. Things went wrong in ways worth telling: an invention that was true, chapters 30 s
   late, a silent render at 2 a.m., a locked Mac, a post published without my edits.
8. It cost about 51 dollars of model and two and a half days of clock, a fraction of it
   my attention.
9. The same episode is now a recipe: the next one starts from data, layout and style,
   and episode 1 is the test that the recipe still makes episode 1.
10. Close: the episode's thesis, AI as a vehicle of expression more than a tool, is what
    happened to me while producing it.
