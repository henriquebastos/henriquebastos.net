# Brief: o podcast que era só para postar

## Source

The production of episode 1 of Henrique's podcast, from October 19 to
October 21, 2026, done in one Pi session with one coding agent. The evidence
lives in the podcast project:

- `~/me/publishing/podcast/retro/ep01/facts/slice-NN.md`: events extracted
  from the session, with line references to the session file.
- The conduit `~/me/vault/Conduits/2026-10-19 Podcast Henrique Bastos/`, and in
  it `01-expand/linha-do-tempo.md`: one timeline by phase, cross-checked
  against commits and file dates.
- The episode folder `episodes/2026-10-18-conversa-alisson/`.

The podcast project owns those facts. This directory owns the blog story.

## Why

Henrique recorded a conversation with Alisson Vale on Zoom with no plan beyond
"vamos compartilhar aqui e dane-se". He then asked himself whether AI would
make a difference and handed the material to an agent. The result was a full
publication package he says he could not have produced alone, for lack of
talent, capacity and time. He wants to share that experience as it happened.

## Promise and audience

Developers and creators who use coding agents and wonder how far they go
outside code. The post tells what happened in two and a half days: what
Henrique asked, what the agent did, which tools came in and why, what went
wrong, and how the two of them talked. The reader leaves with a concrete
picture, not a method.

## Voice

Not teaching, sharing. First person, anti-hype, excitement tied to specific
facts. Failures stay in: the invented guest detail, the hours of render, the
locked Mac, the publication without Henrique's corrections. Numbers are real
and come from the session: about US$ 51 of model cost, 779 agent responses,
72 messages from Henrique.

## Boundaries

- Not a tutorial and not a command log. The logic of the process, in order.
- The recipe (skills for the next episodes) is a separate piece of work. The
  post can mention it as what comes next, without describing what does not
  exist yet.
- Not about the episode's content. That is the published post
  "Eu não quero morar dentro do seu produto".

## Decisions

- Written in `pt-BR` first, then translated into English.
- No Zettelkasten IDs in the post.
