# Brief: o podcast que era só para postar

## Source

The production of episode 1 of Henrique's podcast, from October 19 to
October 21, 2026, and the work right after it that turned the episode into a
recipe for the next ones (October 21 to 22), all in one Pi session with one
coding agent. The evidence lives in the podcast project:

- `~/me/publishing/podcast/episodes/ep01/retro/facts/slice-NN.md`: events
  extracted from the session up to the publication.
- The podcast repository's Git history: the recipe's turns, from the first
  skills to the episode folder organized by purpose.
- The conduit `~/me/vault/Conduits/2026-10-19 Podcast Henrique Bastos/`, and in
  it `01-expand/linha-do-tempo.md`: one timeline by phase, cross-checked
  against commits and file dates.
- The episode folder `~/me/publishing/podcast/episodes/ep01/`.

The podcast project owns those facts. This directory owns the blog story.

## Why

Henrique recorded a conversation with Alisson Vale on Zoom with no plan beyond
"vamos compartilhar aqui e dane-se". He then asked himself whether AI would
make a difference and handed the material to an agent. The result was a full
publication package he says he could not have produced alone, for lack of
talent, capacity and time. He wants to share that experience as it happened.

## Promise and audience

Developers and creators who use coding agents and wonder how far they go
outside code. The post tells it in two acts. Act 1: the episode made by hand in
two and a half days, what Henrique asked, what the agent did, which tools came
in and why, what went wrong, and how the two of them talked. Act 2: making the
next episode cheap, with its own wrong turns. The reader leaves with a concrete
picture, not a method.

## Voice

Not teaching, sharing. First person, anti-hype, excitement tied to specific
facts. Failures stay in: the invented guest detail, the hours of render, the
locked Mac, the publication without Henrique's corrections.

A story, not a scientific report (Henrique, 2026-10-22). Carry the reader
lightly through what happened and what worked, with concrete examples. Numbers
are rounded and serve the point: about 50 dollars, some 70 messages, seven hours
of his attention. Drop details that only prove accuracy, such as byte-for-byte
claims, hashes and exact counts.

## Boundaries

- Not a tutorial and not a command log. The logic of the process, in order.
- The recipe is act 2: told as a story of decisions and wrong turns, not as
  documentation of the skills.
- Not about the episode's content. That is the published post
  "Eu não quero morar dentro do seu produto".

## Decisions
