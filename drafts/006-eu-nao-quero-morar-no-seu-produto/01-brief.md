# Brief: Eu não quero morar dentro do seu produto

## Source

Blog version of the base text for episode 1 of Henrique's podcast, a recorded
conversation with Alisson Vale published on YouTube on October 21, 2026
(<https://youtu.be/2VQ58dqC12k>). The base text lives in the podcast project at
`~/me/publishing/podcast/episodes/2026-10-18-conversa-alisson/textos/texto-base.md`,
and Henrique reviewed and edited it on October 21, 2026. The podcast project
owns that text; this directory owns the blog edit.

## Promise and audience

Brazilian developers and software leaders who use AI products every day and
feel the friction of adapting to someone else's workflow. The post tells what
Henrique built instead: small agents as commands, a timeline of facts instead of
stored conclusions, and the open problem of working together with people who
think differently.

## Decisions

- Written in `pt-BR` from the start. There is no English version yet.
- Changes from the base text: the title, hook headings instead of the base
  text's "X, não Y" labels, a sentence that introduces Alisson, and links to
  Amp, Pi, Alisson's site, the episode and Segue o Flow.
- The episode is a link, not an embed. No existing post embeds a video, so an
  embed is a decision for the rendered-review stage.
