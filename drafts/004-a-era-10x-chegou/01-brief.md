# Brief: A era 10x chegou

## Source

Brazilian Portuguese translation of
[`the-10x-era-is-here.md`](../../src/content/post/the-10x-era-is-here.md), based
on the reviewed article published on August 19, 2026 rather than its earlier
working draft.

## Promise and audience

Preserve the English article's argument, first-person authority, examples, and
humor for Brazilian software leaders and developers. Use the vocabulary common
to Brazilian engineering teams where a forced translation would sound less
natural, including roadmap, ticket, branch, pull request, pipeline, merge,
feature flag, low-code, no-code, and product-market fit.

## Translation decisions

- Portuguese is `pt-BR`, not a generic or European Portuguese locale.
- The public path is `/pt/artigos/a-era-10x-chegou/`.
- "100 mph" becomes "160 km/h" in the PR heading.
- "Deep Engineering™" becomes "Engenharia Profunda™".
- "Free your mind" becomes "Liberte sua mente", preserving the Matrix
  reference.
- Both versions share the same cover: an overwhelmed developer surrounded by
  dark pull request screens and keyboards, with motion trails showing his head
  and hands moving between them.
